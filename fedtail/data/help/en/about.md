# About

The principles fedtail grows from, who wrote it and under which licence.

## Principles

<!-- include: parts/principles -->
1. **Nothing jumps.** Open a lane and you stand at the first unread. New posts come and your
   place doesn't move, only the counter grows. You read in order, like RSS.
2. **Every reply knows what it answers.** Over a reply, a strip with the start of the original
   post; under someone's comment, what you said back to it: come back to a thread a week later
   and you see at once where you were. A click opens the whole thread as a tree.
3. **Lanes are apart.** People apart, bots apart, replies to me apart from other mentions.
4. **Private by default.** A new post goes to followers only. A reply is never more public than
   the original.
5. **Every behaviour is a config key.** There's no settings window and there won't be. The
   defaults are as the author likes them; "like everyone else" is a flag away. New features
   come switchable.
6. **Other servers get no token of yours.** Whatever fedtail takes straight from the author's
   server over ActivityPub goes anonymously: they see only your IP.
7. **The core knows nothing of a GUI.** Interfaces are plugins: a Qt window, a terminal, a
   browser; GTK next.
8. **One profile, one account.** A second account is a second profile (`--profile`); if you
   like, all accounts come together in one window (`accounts.merge`).
9. **Grown-ups sort things out themselves.** Arts mode (after @arts, the founder of Point.im):
   everything as the author posted it, no nannying. Anyone can be told where to go, an admin
   too — and the admin can say the same back.

## Only in fedtail

What none of the thirteen clients we compared with has ([the whole matrix](ref/comparison.md)):

<!-- include: parts/only-in-fedtail -->
- **Lanes:** Bots in a lane of their own · Who is in a list leaves the general lanes · Into a list — the author or the booster; only people you follow · Into a list right after a follow · A tag as its author writes it, as on Point · An empty live lane says why it's empty · Filtering a lane by media and boosts · Repeated boosts don't bring an old post up again · Find in a lane · Sending to a slow server without doubles · Find in every lane at once · Free lanes: a lane you assemble yourself · Federated and tags wider than your server · The lane editor · “Unsorted”: no post gets lost · Sets of lanes “as in other clients” · “To me”: replies and mentions in one lane · Home's quick filters: tags, boosts, threads with news · Important people · Lanes of rules: order by counts, “what did I miss”, “Why is it here?” · A library of pictures for your own lanes
- **Threads:** A whole thread: replies from the author's server · Thread subscriptions, as on Point · A thread with new replies shows on its card · Where a thread opens: at the post or at the first new comment
- **Read marks and place:** Oldest on top, nothing jumps · Catching up with no holes · When a post counts as read · Read marks, thread subscriptions and lane order shared by all your devices · Only the cards near the screen are in memory · Esc draws a curtain instead of closing the window · A draft's pictures travel with it · Sync set up in the window step by step, a new device by a password alone
- **The post card:** Arts mode · Honest counters · The numbers under the posts on screen kept fresh
- **Pictures and video:** Video without a hardware decoder (the default) · Pictures and video ahead of time, a disk cache
- **Profiles:** Posts older than my server knows · “Following since …”
- **Writing:** Visibility: private by default, a reply never wider than the post · CamelCase hint for hashtags · Call someone into a thread · Reply with quotes, piece by piece · A command line as on Point · Short post names the same on every device
- **Notifications and activity:** Desktop notifications with privacy levels · Approve a reply, like, boost (GoToSocial) · Direct messages read here are read on the server too · Activity's quick filters · Who stopped following you · Activity says whom you followed · Someone new in a private talk — shown at once · Slow mode and “notify at most every…” · “Of them new”: what the last update brought
- **The application:** `fedtail --check`: what the system lacks — and one apt line
