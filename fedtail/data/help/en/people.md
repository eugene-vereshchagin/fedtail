# People and quiet

Profiles, follows, mutes, blocks, filters.

## Profiles

A click on a name or an avatar opens the profile in a tab: the header, bio, posts, who follows
them and whom they follow. If your server knows almost nothing about someone from another
server, fedtail reads their posts straight from there, without your token. On the profile of
someone you follow — "without their boosts", "languages" and the bell (each of their posts as a
notification). "Note…" — seen by you only.

## Follows and requests

"Follow" — on a profile, in the search results or with `S @nick`. Who asks to follow you — in
the "Follow requests" lane. fedtail writes into your private note since when you follow
someone: servers don't keep that.

## For the tender and sensitive

≡ → "For the tender and sensitive": word filters, muted, blocked, blocked servers and filtered
notifications. Mute or block someone — the ⋯ menu of a post or their profile. Report to the
moderators — there too (arts mode has no such item).
